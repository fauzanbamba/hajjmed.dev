# Release meeting agenda

## Purpose
Confirm the production launch readiness of the HajjMed 2027 platform and formally record the GO / NO-GO decision.

## Agenda

### 1. Welcome and objective
- Confirm meeting purpose and scope
- Confirm attendees and responsibilities

### 2. Technical readiness review
- Infrastructure status
- API and database status
- Authentication and authorization review
- Backup, restore and migration readiness
- Monitoring and alerting status

### 3. Security and privacy review
- Threat model summary
- Penetration test status
- Remediation status
- DPIA and compliance status

### 4. Clinical readiness review
- Clinical rule validation summary
- Safety findings and closure status
- Medical Director approval status

### 5. Operations and support review
- Incident response readiness
- Runbook and escalation plan
- Monitoring ownership
- Recovery drill summary

### 6. Release gate check
- Environment validation
- Production secrets verification
- Demo mode disabled check
- Release gate command status
- Open risks and actions

### 7. Decision point
- Review final evidence
- Determine GO or NO-GO
- Record conditions and residual risks

### 8. Closeout
- Assign owners for follow-up actions
- Record final approval signatures
- Communicate final decision to all stakeholders

## Required attendees

- Product owner
- Clinical lead / Medical Director
- Security lead
- Privacy / compliance lead
- Infrastructure lead
- Operations lead
- Release authority
- Engineering lead

# Document control and executive summary

## Purpose

HajjMed 2027 is intended to support mandatory pre-Hajj medical screening, vaccination, regional appointment booking, longitudinal clinical care and emergency operations for Ghanaian pilgrims in Ghana and Saudi Arabia.

## Ownership and approval

- Named product owner: Dr. Abdul Samed Sulemana, sole proprietor of NADMED Consult.
- Clinical policy owner: to be formally appointed by the Medical Directorate.
- Data controller, data processor, hosting authority and operational sponsor: to be established by signed governance instruments before production.
- The displayed generic certificate signature is a product requirement; its legal authorization and controlled use require written approval.

## Document status

This documentation describes version 0.2 of the development foundation as at 4 September 2026. Changes must be recorded in source control and reviewed through the change-control process.

## Intended users

Pilgrims, accredited agents, nurses, doctors, administrators and the Medical Director. Permissions are role-based and patient/agency scoped.

## Safety statement

The software supports—not replaces—professional judgement. Screening classifications, triage prioritization and certificate rules require formal clinical validation. No unvalidated rule may make an autonomous diagnosis, deny care or finally determine fitness to travel.

## Status terminology

- **Implemented:** present in executable source and verified by a relevant check.
- **Prototype:** visible interaction using browser-local demonstration state.
- **Foundation:** production-shaped code requiring additional integration/hardening.
- **Planned:** specified but not implemented.
- **Release gate:** evidence required before real-world use.
